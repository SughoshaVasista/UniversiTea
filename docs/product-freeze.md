# UniversiTea Product Freeze

## Implemented

- Public browsing across communities with registered-account participation.
- Contextual anonymous identities with private account mappings.
- Posts, threaded comments, post voting, receipts, claims, moderation, and notifications.
- Personalized home feed with explainable ranking, follows, saves, and private mutes.
- Claim hubs and timelines.
- Comment voting, anonymous profile settings, and Light/Dark/System appearance controls.

## Partially implemented

- Receipt redaction/versioning and claim expiration need production storage and moderator workflow validation.
- Digest delivery, advanced notification grouping, and full community wiki governance are staged for post-beta hardening.

## Planned

- Production observability dashboards, measured latency budgets, restore drills, and a managed upload pipeline.

## Abandoned

- College email verification, student-ID verification, and public global anonymous identity lookup.

The product freeze protects the core model: anyone may browse, any normal UniversiTea account may participate, and college affiliation is not an access requirement.
